Wallet and Plutus

    
Plutus checks the inventory and the DDMRP buffers, orders, sends the orders to the
      suppliers, approves and places baskets, and pays matched bills. It spends only from this wallet, which
      only a person funds, and inside the limits in Rules for the two bots. It starts off, then in dry run
      (it writes down what it would do and does nothing), and can go live after 3 days.
      The wallet is this program's own record: a payment is instructed into a bank file, and a bill turns paid
      when you confirm the bank took it.

Policies

    
A policy lets routine work go through without a person: send an order, send a
      reminder, or approve a held basket. Everything is off until the kill switch is on. A new policy is
      inactive and in dry-run, which only writes down what it would have done; it can go live after
      3 days of that. No policy can pass 5000 EUR
      in one action, act outside working days and its hours, send an order whose price cannot be checked,
      or approve a basket Terminus did not read as the same.

Rules for the two bots

    
What Plutus (the buyer) and Terminus (the name reader) are free to do and what they
      must never do. Both go to the model with its instructions, and the limits are applied again in code
      to whatever it answers.

    

      
      
      
    

    

    

    

    

  


  

    
Calendar, supplier terms, rates

    
French public holidays are known. Add your own closures, and per supplier the
      hour after which an order counts as the next day, the weekdays it is shut (0 Monday to 6 Sunday),
      and its currency. Bills in another currency are checked in euros at the rate for their day.
