Safety stock

Lead days is how long the supplier takes. Service % is how often you want to be covered when a week runs hot. Safety stock is the service factor times the week to week swing over the lead time; the reorder point is the lead time demand on top of it.

On hand is yours to fill in: type a count and it is saved against the article, dated. Without it the shelf is a guess, and the article is marked not counted.
